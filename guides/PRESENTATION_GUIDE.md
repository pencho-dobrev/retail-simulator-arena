# Presentation guide

Each team gives a short presentation (8 minutes) about its experience in the competition. Cover
the five parts below, in this order.

## 1. Your team

- Introduce yourselves briefly and say who did what.

## 2. Your strategy hypothesis

- Before you trained anything, what did you expect would win in this market? Which levers did you
  think would matter most, and why?
- Say where the idea came from: the rules, a quick experiment, watching the practice NPCs, or
  intuition.

## 3. Your experiments

- What you tried: algorithms, settings, rewards, training opponents.
- Show at least one chart or table as evidence, for example a training curve or two candidates
  compared in a tournament.
- Say what each experiment taught you, including the ones that didn't work.

## 4. One thing that went wrong

- Pick one specific failure, not a general difficulty like "training was slow".
- Describe the symptom, how you worked out the cause, and what you changed.

## 5. The agent you submitted

- Why you chose this checkpoint over your other candidates.
- How it actually plays: how many stores it opens, how it sets prices, how much it spends on
  marketing. Play a self-check match and read its decision log to find out.
- How you checked it before submitting.
